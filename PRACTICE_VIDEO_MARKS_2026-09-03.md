## Fix list from this session

7a. (pinned 2026-09-03 evening; DIAGNOSED during the smoke pass, NOT fixed,
   PRE-EXISTING — not from today's batch) The "garbled long title" in the
   document viewer's top-left corner is the LIBRARY SCREEN bleeding through:
   expo-router keeps the previous screen mounted (warm screens), and on web
   that inactive screen collapses to a 0×0 box at the origin with overflow
   visible — its texts ("Daily routine", "My Library", card titles) paint
   into the corner. Long unbroken filenames stick out past the real header,
   short words hide under it. Fix belongs at the router/screen-hiding level
   (affects every screen) — own session, verify against live first.
7. Prompt controls asymmetry: banners fire on ANY screen (library included) but the timer tool + toggle only exist on practice screens — no way to silence prompts where they appear. Either mount the timer tools where banners can fire, or give the banner itself a tiny mute affordance.
8. Exercise Builder: a saved exercise should remember its starting tempo (his canonical start, not wherever the dial drifted mid-session — he asked for "what I set it to the first time"). Design decision needed: first-set vs. last-confirmed-start; either beats forgetting.
9. Drone control clarity: the toggle reads "Off" but looks like a plain button — ambiguous whether pressing turns it on or off. Make state vs. action unmistakable (lit/unlit chip like RHYTHMS, or a real switch).
10. Drone pitch memory: remember the chosen drone pitch per passage/exercise (fourth member of the "remember what the player set" family — see #8).
11. Practice-log legibility: ICU entries say "two passes" — its own author couldn't tell a day later whether that meant forward+backward. Log entries should describe the session in reconstructable player language (what a pass traversed, direction, units). Check what "pass" actually counts in the ICU code before rewording.
12. Drone audio: intermittent click, worse at faster tempos, comes and goes (iPad web). Tempo correlation points at drone × click-scheduling/gain-envelope interaction. ⚠️ Read the F30 audio-idle memory and use ?audiodebug=1 before touching web audio.

Lift take state (blob + playback state) out of the card component to session level. Both halves wanted: the take surviving AND play-kills-other-audio removing the need to leave at all.
